Privacy

Privacy notice.

What this site does with data, and what it deliberately does not collect. Last updated 27 September 2026.

Who is responsible

Marcus Maute, Zürich, Switzerland. Contact: marcus@marcusmaute.com. This notice covers marcusmaute.com.

What this site does not do

It sets no cookies, runs no advertising and has no forms, no accounts and no newsletter. Its fonts are self-hosted in assets/fonts rather than loaded from Google Fonts or any other font service, and its images are served from this domain. Nothing else is loaded from a third party except the analytics beacon described below.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages (Cloudflare, Inc.). To deliver pages and protect the service, Cloudflare processes connection data such as IP addresses, the time of a request and the browser's user agent. Cloudflare may process this data outside Switzerland, including in the United States, under its own data processing terms.

Measurement

Since 27 September 2026 the site counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is why a script loads from static.cloudflareinsights.com. It is cookieless: it stores no identifier on your device and does not follow you across other sites. What it produces is aggregate only: page views, the referring site, country, browser and how quickly pages render.

Your choice of view

The Human/Agent toggle at the top of every page remembers your choice in your own browser, under the local storage key mm-mode. It never leaves your device, and clearing this site's data in your browser removes it.

Email

Mail sent to the address above is stored with the mail provider and kept for as long as needed to answer it.

Your rights

Under Swiss data protection law, and the GDPR where it applies, you may ask what is held about you and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Write to the address above.
