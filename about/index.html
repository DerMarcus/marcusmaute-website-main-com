Who I am

I represent TensorX in Switzerland and work at the intersection of sovereign AI, digital-asset infrastructure and regulated finance. How I work
The mandate first.

Every layer of the agentic stack can be bought: regulated custody, settlement in seconds, sovereign inference, orchestration, identity. The mandate cannot. It is the document that says what an agent may do, within what limits, who confirms what, and where the line sits that the agent cannot cross, and it has to be enforced somewhere the agent cannot reach. Writing it well is organisational work, and it is the work most programmes skip on the way to buying a model.

That is the position I take in the report and in every engagement: the technical deployment takes weeks, the governance takes months, and the first step needs no technology at all.
