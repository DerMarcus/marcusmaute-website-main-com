The Mandate Workshop

For a treasurer, COO or head of operations and the people who will own the first agent. We take the report's framework and write your institution's first mandate against it, on a real cash pool.

You leave with a document your risk committee can read: the permitted universe, the allowlists, the caps, the three tiers and where their boundaries sit, the escalation path, the revocation mechanism, and the audit requirement. Plus the list of what has to be enforced in infrastructure rather than in a prompt, and who supplies each piece.

  • Day 1 · Where the capital waits, and the loop it should run
  • Day 2 · The mandate, tier by tier, written and reviewed
  • Output · The mandate, the gap list, the pilot scope

Readiness assessment

The report's five preconditions applied to one institution: is the asset held by a regulated institution, does the rail settle finally and cheaply, does the reasoning run where you can see it, is every decision attributable, is the agent identifiable as acting for you.

A written gap list, the order to close it in, and an honest view of which pieces are in production today and which are still forward-looking. Often the step before the workshop.

  • Input · Your treasury set-up, your custodian, your constraints
  • Output · A gap list against the five preconditions
  • Follow-on · The Mandate Workshop, or nothing, honestly
